## One set, many ways to study

Studo doesn't force every subject into one generic method. Each set can be studied through the mode that fits the material best, so you spend your time actively learning instead of passively rereading.

## Text & definition modes

For **Studosets** — term/definition pairs, with support for LaTeX formulas, code and images:

- **Learn** — spaced repetition with a queue that keeps going until everything sticks. It scales with your level and includes Health Mode (the 20‑20‑2 rule) and a built-in pomodoro timer.
- **Speedy** — a dynamic time limit based on the length of each card. Answer correctly to clear cards from the queue; get everything right in as few rounds as possible.
- **Flashcards** — classic cards that flip to reveal the other side.
- **Print** — a clean, printable list of your cards for offline study.

## Visual modes

For **Visualsets** — images with pins linked to definitions, ideal for anatomy, biology or geography:

- **Spot** — the system walks through every pin, you type the correct definition. Wrong answers return to the queue until everything is learned.
- **Point** — the reverse: Studo shows a definition, you point to the right pin.

## Built to grow with you

The spaced-repetition algorithm adapts as you improve, so the curve stays challenging without becoming overwhelming — smarter, not harder.
