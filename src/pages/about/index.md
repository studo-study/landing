## The problem

For nearly every student, studying is a time-consuming, repetitive ritual. Study loads keep growing while the time to learn them stays fixed, and the pressure climbs year after year. The tools meant to help are fragmented: students juggle 3 to 5 apps — one for vocabulary, one for spaced repetition, one for notes, loose PDFs for diagrams.

On top of that, most of those tools aren't built for higher education. There's poor support for LaTeX formulas, images tied to definitions, complex diagrams, or spaced-repetition algorithms that actually grow with you. And for **visual learning** — anatomy, schematics, processes — there's barely anything at all.

## Our vision

> Studo wants to change the way students in higher education study: from fragmented and time-consuming to integrated, adaptive and efficient.

## Our mission

Studo combines proven learning methods in one platform that adapts to the student — not the other way around. We help students **study smarter, not harder.**

## The solution

Studo is an all-in-one smart learning app that:

- Encourages active learning
- Automates revision through spaced repetition
- Offers the right study method per type of material
- Combines methods for maximum results
- Grows with the student
- Keeps you motivated with streaks

Everything revolves around one central idea: **the subject**. A subject bundles your material (sets) and your follow-up (Flow) for one topic in one place — so your whole semester stays in view, not just the week before the exam.
