> This is a plain-language summary while our full legal policy is finalised. It is not yet a binding legal document.

## Our approach

Studo is built privacy-first. We collect only what we need to run the product, and every social and progress-sharing feature is optional and controllable by you.

## What we collect

- **Account data** — the details you provide when you register (name, email, login).
- **Study content** — the sets, subjects and Flow items you create.
- **Usage data** — how you interact with the app, to improve study modes and fix issues.

## What we don't do

- We don't sell your personal data.
- We don't track progress inside groups unless you switch it on.
- We don't publish AI-generated content without your explicit approval.

## Advertising

The free tier shows contextual, non-intrusive ads related to study material. Ads never appear inside focused learning modes, and **Studo Select** removes them entirely.

## Your rights

You can access, correct and delete your data at any time. For details on GDPR rights, see the [GDPR page](/gdpr). Questions? [Contact us](/contact).
