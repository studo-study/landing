## Learn together

Studying doesn't have to be a solo effort. Groups are shared learning spaces where you can share material, run challenges and keep each other on track.

## Three kinds of spaces

- **Classrooms (Official)** — class groups tied to a school or institution, created by teachers or professors. Students join by invite or code. Built around subjects, courses and exams.
- **Study Groups** — private or semi-private groups made by students, for friends or project teams. Flexible and informal.
- **Community** — open, public spaces for everyone, focused on sharing study material at scale.

## What you can do

Across every kind of group you can share Studosets and visualsets and pin the ones that matter. Inside Classrooms and Study Groups you also get:

- **Progress tracking** — optional and fully privacy-controlled.
- **Competitions & challenges** — one student starts, the rest join in.
- **Shared statistics** — compare results on shared sets, or switch it off entirely.

Privacy stays in your hands: every tracking and comparison feature can be disabled.
