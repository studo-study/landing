## Getting started

New to Studo? Here's the short version:

1. **Create a set** — add terms and definitions manually, or import from Word, Excel or copy-paste. You can add LaTeX formulas, code and images too.
2. **Link it to a subject** — just type a subject name. If it exists, your set links to it; if not, a new subject is created automatically. No extra steps.
3. **Study** — open a [study mode](/studymodes) that fits the material and start learning.

## Organising your work

- **Subjects** bundle your sets and your Flow follow-up for one topic.
- **Boards** optionally group subjects per semester — drag and drop a subject in whenever you like.
- **Flow** is a table inside each subject where you capture lessons, notes, summaries, exercises and tasks, and track their status from *not started* to *done*.

## Studying with others

Share sets, run challenges and track progress inside [Groups](/groups) — classrooms, study groups or open communities.
