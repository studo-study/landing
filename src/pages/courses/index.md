## What are Courses?

Courses are ready-made study tracks built and maintained by the Studo team, so you always start from reliable, high-quality material instead of a blank page. Each course bundles everything around a single topic, exam or study programme.

## Tracks & structure

Every course is split into **tracks** — modules that group related subjects together. Inside each track you'll find the relevant Studosets and visualsets, made by Studo, ready to study straight away.

- **Courses** — the full learning path around a subject or exam.
- **Tracks** — modules within a course.
- **Sets** — the actual study material inside each track.

## Verified Sets

Not everything has to come from us. Community sets can earn a **"Verified by Studo"** badge when they are checked by our team, proven to hit 95%+ exam accuracy, or approved by a teacher or professor. Verified sets get priority in search results and are featured on subject pages.

> Courses and Verified Sets arrive in phase 2 of the Studo roadmap.
