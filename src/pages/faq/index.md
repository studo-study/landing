## Is Studo free?

Yes. The core of Studo — Studosets, visualsets and groups — is free to use. A premium tier, **Studo Select**, adds an AI layer (SVEN) on top for those who want it.

## Who is Studo for?

Studo is built for higher-education students, with an early focus on content-heavy fields like medicine, biomedical sciences, physiotherapy and STEM — anyone who has to memorise a lot of visual material such as diagrams, anatomy, processes and classifications. It also works great for the final years of secondary school and for self-study.

## What makes Studo different?

Most tools force everything into one generic method. Studo combines multiple proven methods in a single platform, offers real support for LaTeX, code, images and visual learning, and uses a spaced-repetition algorithm that grows with you.

## What are sets, subjects and Flow?

A **set** is your study material (term/definition or visual). A **subject** bundles all the sets and follow-up for one topic. **Flow** is the structured follow-up system inside a subject that helps you track lessons, summaries and tasks — so you keep coming back between exam months, not just during them.

## What languages does Studo support?

From launch, Studo is available in English, Dutch, French and German.

## Which platforms can I use?

Studo is a web app, so it runs anywhere with a browser.
