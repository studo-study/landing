## Your rights

Under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), you have the right to:

- **Access** — request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
- **Rectification** — correct data that is inaccurate or incomplete.
- **Erasure** — ask us to delete your personal data ("right to be forgotten").
- **Restriction** — limit how we process your data.
- **Portability** — receive your data in a portable format.
- **Objection** — object to certain kinds of processing, including advertising.

## Lawful basis

We process personal data to provide the Studo service (contractual necessity), to improve the product (legitimate interest), and — where required — with your consent.

## Data location

Studo runs on EU-based infrastructure. Study material and images are stored with providers chosen for security and compliance.

## Exercising your rights

To make any request above, email [hello@studo.study](mailto:hello@studo.study). We'll respond within the timeframes required by the GDPR. See also our [Privacy Notice](/privacy).
